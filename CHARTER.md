# Charter

## Purpose

Renaissance exists to increase humanity's ability to **understand, explore, create, and flourish**.

The project is intended to develop open, rigorous, durable tools and infrastructure that help people discover what is true, investigate what is unknown, explore what is possible, learn, create, and cooperate.

Humanity is the beneficiary and protagonist of the project.

The machinery exists to serve that purpose.

## Scope

Renaissance is a developing constellation of principles, architecture, software, research, protocols, and projects.

The exact scope is intentionally not fixed at the founding stage.

## Non-goals

Renaissance is not established as:

- a system of government;
- a substitute for human judgment;
- a single mandatory worldview;
- an authority that determines what humanity must believe;
- a system whose own continuation is an end in itself.

These boundaries may be refined through the constitutional process.
