# Decision 0002 — Tightening Authority and Terminology

## Status

**Ratified during the founding phase.**

## Matter

The founding corpus requires a small amount of procedural and terminological tightening before the initial baseline is frozen.

The changes address:

1. mandatory decision records for substantive and constitutional changes;
2. an explicit authority hierarchy;
3. clarification of “open”;
4. clarification of human agency terminology.

## Prior state

The initial foundation established deliberate change control, a founding Human Gate, and an intentionally incomplete constitutional framework.

It did not yet make decision records mandatory for all substantive and constitutional changes, explicitly distinguish levels of authority, or define the limits of the terms above.

## Decision

The following are established:

### 1. Decision records

Substantive and constitutional changes **MUST** have a decision record under `DECISIONS/`.

### 2. Authority hierarchy

The project recognizes this order:

1. Constitutional Canon
2. Foundational Canon established by the Charter and ratified Decisions
3. Established requirements and architecture
4. Implementation
5. Proposals and experiments

Lower-level material may not silently override higher-level canon.

### 3. Open

“Open” describes the project's intended orientation toward inspectability, participation, interoperability, and forkability.

It does not itself establish a specific license, legal structure, access policy, or intellectual-property regime.

### 4. Human agency

Human agency means the capacity of people to make, revise, reject, and act upon their own judgments and choices.

The project does not thereby acquire an inherent authority to decide which human choices are “legitimate.” Any future limitation or qualification must be explicitly established through the governing framework.

## Affected documents

- `CHANGE_CONTROL.md`
- `GOVERNANCE.md`
- `GLOSSARY.md`

## Rationale

These changes close procedural and semantic loopholes without expanding the project's substantive mission or pretending that unresolved future governance questions have already been answered.

## Unresolved matters

Licensing, long-term governance, amendment thresholds, legal structure, security/privacy policy, autonomy policy, and other matters deliberately left unresolved by Decision 0001 remain unresolved.
