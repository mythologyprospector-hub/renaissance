# Decision 0007 — Accept Renaissance Constellation Shape

**Date:** 2026-09-29  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Authority:** Human Gate  
**Scope:** Renaissance constellation and cross-project architectural shape

## Matter

Determine whether the current Renaissance constellation model accurately represents the intended shape of the project after the completed archaeology and architectural review.

## Prior state

The Renaissance repository established a constitutional foundation and a provisional constellation audit. Subsequent work examined the relationships among Renaissance, Episteme, Tiger Den, AI Foundry, and Organs, while deliberately preserving the independence of projects whose concrete relationships were not yet established.

The constellation audit remained a proposed working document pending Human Gate review.

## Evidence

The current evidence establishes:

- Renaissance provides the constitutional and architectural foundation.
- Episteme provides a concrete inquiry/discovery capability while retaining independent project identity.
- Tiger Den provides a distinct computational discovery/mapping capability while retaining independent project identity.
- AI Foundry provides an engineering and experimentation laboratory while retaining independent project identity.
- Organs provides reusable runtime infrastructure and does not constitute epistemic or constitutional authority.
- Learn remains a recognized human-facing capability gap whose implementation has not been prematurely defined.
- Decision 0006 establishes a minimum relationship-interoperability boundary without selecting a universal protocol or ontology.
- The completed archaeology found no requirement to collapse these projects into one repository or impose sameness of implementation.

## Decision

Renaissance **accepts the current constellation shape as the intended working architectural model**:

- Renaissance is the constitutional foundation.
- Episteme and Tiger Den are distinct capability/inquiry projects within the wider constellation.
- AI Foundry is an independent experimental and engineering laboratory within the wider constellation.
- Organs is reusable runtime infrastructure beneath capabilities and instruments.
- Learn remains an identified capability gap to be explored through evidence rather than prematurely implemented.
- Other independent or specialist repositories remain independent unless a concrete architectural relationship is later established.

The governing principle is:

> **Unity without sameness.**

Acceptance of the constellation does not require repository absorption, shared implementation, identical internal models, or immediate interoperability work.

## Authorization boundary

This decision authorizes the constellation shape as an architectural working model.

It does **not**:

- establish final interfaces between projects;
- require a universal runtime or data model;
- require a Learn repository;
- promote every audit classification to constitutional canon;
- establish ownership over independent projects;
- select a final interoperability protocol;
- require repository renaming, relocation, deletion, or rebranding.

Future substantive boundary decisions remain subject to the established change-control process.

## Relationship to existing authority

This decision accepts the working model developed through the constellation audit and subsequent archaeology.

It does not supersede the Constitution, Charter, existing Decisions, or established project boundaries. The constellation audit remains useful as an inventory and evidence record; its working classifications should not be treated as stronger authority than this decision establishes.

## Historical preservation

The audit, experiment records, prior proposals, and earlier decisions remain part of Renaissance history and evidence. Acceptance of the constellation shape does not erase or rewrite those records.
