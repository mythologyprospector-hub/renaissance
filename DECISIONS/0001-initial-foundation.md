# Decision 0001 — Initial Constitutional Foundation

**Status:** Accepted as the founding baseline  
**Scope:** Renaissance constitutional foundation

## Purpose

Establish the initial constitutional framework for Renaissance without pretending that the project's future governance, architecture, ethics, or capabilities are already completely known.

## Established

1. Renaissance exists to increase humanity's ability to understand, explore, create, and flourish.
2. Human agency is fundamental.
3. No software component possesses inherent sovereignty.
4. Renaissance has no inherent self-preservation mandate.
5. Unknown is a legitimate epistemic state.
6. Important epistemic categories must remain distinguishable.
7. Meaningful provenance should be preserved where practical.
8. Implementation must not silently acquire constitutional authority.
9. Replaceability, interoperability, inspectability, portability, and forkability are foundational architectural goals.
10. Renaissance must retain mechanisms for revision and correction.
11. The project will distinguish canon, proposals, decisions, architecture, implementation, experiments, and history.
12. The constitutional foundation is intentionally incomplete.

## Deliberately unresolved

- final long-term governance structure;
- legal organizational structure;
- complete ethical framework;
- final licensing strategy;
- complete security and privacy framework;
- complete technical architecture;
- full autonomy policy;
- final amendment thresholds;
- complete contributor authority model;
- the complete set of future Renaissance capabilities.

## Effect

This decision establishes the initial constitutional baseline for Renaissance.

Future decisions may clarify, amend, extend, or supersede portions of this baseline. Such changes should preserve the project's historical record.
