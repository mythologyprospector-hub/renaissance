# Decision 0008 — Clarify Independent Project Boundaries

**Date:** 2026-09-29  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Authority:** Human Gate  
**Scope:** Renaissance project boundary and current working constellation

## Matter

Determine whether Tiger Den and AI Foundry should remain represented as projects within the Renaissance working constellation after clarification of their provenance and intended project boundaries.

## Prior state

Decision 0007 accepted a Renaissance constellation model that included Tiger Den and AI Foundry as distinct projects within the wider constellation while preserving their independent identities.

Subsequent clarification established that both projects predate Renaissance, originated independently of the Renaissance architectural effort, and are being maintained separately from Renaissance. Their current project-management disposition does not establish a Renaissance architectural relationship.

## Evidence

The current evidence establishes:

- Renaissance and Episteme are the products of the later architectural work being continued here.
- Tiger Den and AI Foundry are independent projects that predate that work.
- No concrete Renaissance capability currently requires either project.
- No established Renaissance contract, dependency, ownership relationship, or implementation boundary requires either project to remain in the Renaissance working field.
- The principle **Unity without sameness** does not require unrelated projects to be incorporated merely because their capabilities resemble provisional Renaissance responsibilities.
- Future Renaissance work may independently produce capabilities resembling work found elsewhere; such work should be evaluated on its own evidence rather than retroactively mapped to existing external projects.

## Decision

Renaissance clarifies its current working boundary as follows:

- **Tiger Den is outside the Renaissance working constellation.**
- **AI Foundry is outside the Renaissance working constellation.**
- Neither project is treated as a Renaissance implementation of Explore or Create.
- Renaissance does not depend on either project and will not plan work around either project unless a concrete future architectural relationship is independently established.
- Their independent identity and project boundaries are preserved.
- Renaissance remains free to develop Explore, Create, or other capabilities if genuine work establishes a need for them.
- Similarity to an external project is not, by itself, evidence of architectural membership or required integration.

The governing principle remains:

> **Unity without sameness.**

## Authorization boundary

This decision authorizes correction of Renaissance's current architectural descriptions to reflect the clarified boundary.

It does **not**:

- modify or control the independent repositories;
- require migration, deletion, renaming, or rebranding of those repositories;
- establish replacement projects for Explore or Create;
- require new interfaces, adapters, protocols, or integration work;
- imply that Renaissance must remain symmetrical across its provisional capability model;
- prevent a genuinely new Renaissance capability from emerging later through independent evidence.

## Affected documents

The following Renaissance documents require alignment with this decision:

- README.md
- CAPABILITIES/HUMAN_CAPABILITY_MODEL.md
- ARCHITECTURE.md

Decision 0007 remains historical record of the constellation accepted at that time.

## Supersession

This decision supersedes only the portions of Decision 0007 and related working documents that classify Tiger Den or AI Foundry as members of the Renaissance working constellation.

All other portions of Decision 0007 remain in force, including the acceptance of Renaissance as constitutional foundation, Organs as runtime infrastructure, Learn as an unresolved capability gap, preservation of independent project boundaries, and the principle **Unity without sameness**.

## Historical preservation

Decision 0007 is not rewritten or erased. The earlier constellation remains recoverable as a record of the architectural state accepted on 2026-09-29 before this boundary clarification.

The purpose of this decision is to correct the current working map without rewriting the path by which Renaissance reached it.
