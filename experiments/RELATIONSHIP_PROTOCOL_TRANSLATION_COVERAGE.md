# Relationship Protocol Translation — Coverage

**Status:** Bounded experiment  
**Authority:** Experiment  
**Relationship to canon:** No translation standard is established here.

## Question

Can an experiment distinguish a faithful relationship translation from an unmapped or altered representation without defining a universal translation dictionary?

## Current findings

- A mapped translation can retain source identity, participants, and source/target relationship types.
- The translation record can explicitly distinguish source meaning from target meaning.
- An unmapped relationship fails explicitly rather than silently inventing a mapping.
- Altering participants or source meaning metadata makes the experimental fidelity check fail.

## Deliberate non-conclusions

This experiment does not define semantic equivalence, a universal translation dictionary, a target ontology, a trust model, or a final translation protocol.
