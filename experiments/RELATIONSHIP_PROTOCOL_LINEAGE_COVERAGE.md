# Relationship Protocol Lineage — Coverage

**Status:** Bounded experiment  
**Authority:** Experiment  
**Relationship to canon:** No protocol requirement is established here.

## Question

Can transformation lineage identify the source relationship and distinguish successive transformations without replacing the source assertion?

## Current findings

- A transformation record can identify both source and resulting relationship identities.
- Multiple transformations can remain ordered and distinct.
- Lineage supplements the represented relationship rather than replacing its identity.
- An empty lineage is representable when no transformation is claimed.

## Deliberate non-conclusions

This experiment does not establish a final provenance model, identifier scheme, trust/authentication mechanism, serialization, or mandatory lineage policy.
