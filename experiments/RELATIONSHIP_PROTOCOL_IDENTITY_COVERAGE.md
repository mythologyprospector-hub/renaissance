# Relationship Protocol Identity — Coverage

**Status:** Bounded experiment  
**Authority:** Experiment  
**Relationship to canon:** No identifier requirement is established here.

## Question

What is the smallest experimentally testable identity property needed to keep distinct relationships distinguishable across interoperability boundaries?

## Current findings

- An interoperability representation can preserve an identity value without prescribing its syntax.
- Identity stability can be tested as preservation of the source identity and its originating scope.
- Identical identity text from different origins need not imply the same relationship.
- Changing identity breaks the experimental stability property.

## Deliberate non-conclusions

This experiment does not establish a globally unique identifier scheme, UUID requirement, URI requirement, namespace standard, identifier registry, or final protocol field names.
