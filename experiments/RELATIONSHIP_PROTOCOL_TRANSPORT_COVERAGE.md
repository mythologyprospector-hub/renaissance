# Relationship Protocol Transport — Coverage

**Status:** Bounded experiment  
**Authority:** Experiment  
**Relationship to canon:** No transport standard is established here.

## Question

Can transport metadata remain distinguishable from relationship meaning, epistemic authority, agreement, and source provenance?

## Current findings

- A transported representation can preserve relationship identity, participants, and relationship type.
- Destination information can be represented as transport metadata without replacing relationship meaning.
- Transport does not itself add epistemic authority.
- Transport does not itself establish agreement.
- Transport metadata does not replace source provenance.

## Deliberate non-conclusions

This experiment does not establish HTTP, message queues, federation protocols, APIs, network topology, delivery guarantees, authentication, authorization, or a final wire format.
