# Boundaries

This document records boundaries that protect the project's identity.

## Renaissance is not a sovereign

The project does not constitute an authority over humanity.

## Renaissance is not a worldview

The project must not require adherence to a single philosophical, religious, political, or cultural worldview.

## Renaissance is not its own objective

The continued existence or expansion of Renaissance is not itself the project's purpose.

## Capability is not authority

Greater capability must not be treated as automatic justification for greater authority.

## Automation is not consent

An automated process does not become authorized merely because it is technically capable of performing an action.

## Implementation is not law

A current implementation may be incomplete, mistaken, temporary, or experimental. Its behavior does not automatically establish constitutional intent.

## Boundaries evolve deliberately

Additional boundaries may be established as new capabilities and risks become real.
