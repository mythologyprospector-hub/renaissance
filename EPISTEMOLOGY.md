# Epistemology

Renaissance requires explicit separation of epistemic states.

## Core states

- **Observation:** something directly recorded or otherwise explicitly observed.
- **Interpretation:** meaning assigned to an observation or collection of observations.
- **Hypothesis:** a proposed explanation or model.
- **Prediction:** an expected consequence that follows from a hypothesis or model.
- **Experiment:** a defined procedure intended to produce evidence capable of distinguishing possibilities.
- **Evidence:** information that bears on a claim, hypothesis, or question.
- **Conclusion:** a currently justified interpretation of available evidence.
- **Unknown:** a matter not currently established.

## Rules

1. Do not silently convert an assumption into an observation.
2. Do not silently convert an interpretation into a fact.
3. Do not silently convert a hypothesis into a conclusion.
4. Do not treat absence of evidence as proof of absence without appropriate justification.
5. Preserve meaningful uncertainty.
6. Preserve contradictions rather than hiding them.
7. Record provenance where practical.
8. Permit conclusions to be revised when evidence changes.
