# Change Control

## Principle

Important changes must be deliberate, traceable, and reviewable.

## Change classes

- **Editorial:** corrections that do not change meaning.
- **Clarification:** improves precision without changing established intent.
- **Substantive:** changes an established principle, boundary, definition, authority relationship, or other meaningful requirement.
- **Constitutional:** establishes, removes, or materially changes constitutional canon.

## Decision record

**Substantive and constitutional changes MUST have a decision record under `DECISIONS/`.**

A decision record should identify:

- the matter being decided;
- prior state;
- relevant evidence or reasoning;
- the decision;
- affected documents;
- effective status;
- superseded material, if any.

## No retroactive erasure

Superseded decisions and principles should remain recoverable as historical records.

## Unresolved matters

If a question is not ready for decision, record it as proposed or unresolved rather than forcing a premature conclusion.

## Implementation follows authorization

Implementation should follow established decisions. It must not silently convert an implementation choice into a constitutional rule.
