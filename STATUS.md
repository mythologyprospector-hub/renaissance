# Project Status

**Project:** Renaissance  
**Foundation:** Initial Constitutional Foundation v0.1  
**Status:** Foundational / evolving

## Authority states

- **Canon:** deliberately established and currently authoritative.
- **Proposed:** under consideration; not automatically authoritative.
- **Decision:** a recorded act that establishes, changes, or supersedes canon.
- **Architecture:** the technical expression of established principles and requirements.
- **Implementation:** software or operational machinery implementing the architecture.
- **Experiment:** a bounded exploration; results do not automatically become canon.
- **History:** the record of what was previously established, proposed, changed, or superseded.

## Current baseline

The initial foundation establishes the project's purpose, foundational constraints, basic epistemic distinctions, and a controlled process for future change.

Many future questions remain deliberately unresolved.
