# Glossary

## Canon
Material deliberately established as authoritative within the Renaissance project.

## Constitutional Canon
The highest-authority material establishing the project's foundational boundaries, principles, and authority rules.

## Foundational Canon
Established project-level material that derives its authority from the Charter and ratified Decisions without itself being constitutional canon.

## Proposal
Material under consideration that has not been established as canon.

## Decision
A recorded act establishing, changing, or superseding canon.

## Architecture
The technical and structural expression of established requirements and principles.

## Implementation
A concrete system, program, configuration, or operational practice that implements architecture.

## Experiment
A bounded exploration whose results do not automatically become canon.

## Human Gate
The human authority responsible for foundational approval during the founding phase.

## Human Agency
The capacity of people to make, revise, reject, and act upon their own judgments and choices.

Where the project uses the phrase “legitimate human agency,” “legitimate” does not grant Renaissance an inherent authority to determine which human choices are legitimate. Any future limitation or qualification must be explicitly established through the project's governing framework.

## Provenance
Information describing the origin, lineage, transformation, or history of a claim, artifact, or decision.

## Unknown
A matter that is not currently established.

## Open
In the founding context, “open” describes Renaissance's intended orientation toward inspectability, participation, interoperability, and the ability to build upon and fork its work.

“Open” does not, by itself, establish a particular software license, legal structure, access policy, or intellectual-property regime. Those matters remain subject to explicit future decisions.

## Renaissance
The project defined by the charter and constitutional documents in this repository.
